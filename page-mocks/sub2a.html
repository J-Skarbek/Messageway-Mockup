What to Expect During Your Session

Seven steps to improved flexibility and wellness

1

Consultation & Goals

Your session begins with a brief conversation about your body, lifestyle, and any areas of tension, pain, or limited mobility. This helps your licensed massage therapist customize the session to your specific needs.

2

Get Comfortable

You'll remain fully clothed unless you've added onto another massage service. Sessions typically take place on a massage table, depending on the techniques being used.

3

Guided Stretching Begins

Your licensed massage therapist will gently move your body through a series of assisted stretches, targeting key muscle groups such as the neck, shoulders, back, hips, and legs.

4

Controlled Techniques

Each stretch is performed slowly and safely. Your therapist may incorporate light traction, stabilization, or gentle pressure to deepen the stretch and improve effectiveness.

5

Communication & Breathing

You'll be encouraged to breathe deeply and communicate throughout the session. This ensures the stretch intensity remains comfortable and never painful.

6

Release & Relaxation

As your muscles begin to relax, you may notice increased range of motion and reduced tension throughout your body.

7

Post-Session Benefits

After your session, you can expect to feel more flexible, mobile, and refreshed. Your therapist may also offer simple recommendations or stretches to maintain your results between visits.
