Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any medical conditions I should mention before booking?

Please let us know about severe osteoporosis, blood clots/DVT, serious cardiovascular conditions, skin infections, acute injuries, or severe neuropathy. When in doubt, check with your healthcare provider first.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive at least 10 minutes before your appointment. Late arrivals will result in a shortened session.

Can I request a specific therapist or gender preference?

Absolutely — just let us know when booking and we'll do our best to accommodate your preference.

Do I need to undress for my massage?

Undressing is always optional and at your comfort level. You'll be draped throughout your session regardless.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule; a 50% fee may apply otherwise. A card is required to hold your appointment, and cancellations are handled by calling our studio directly.

What should I do after my massage?

Hydrate well, rest, and consider gentle stretching or heat/cold as needed. We recommend avoiding strenuous exercise for 24 hours and following your therapist's guidance on any topical products.

I just had surgery — can I still book a massage?

Yes, with clearance from your surgeon first. Let us know so we can tailor your post-operative Manual Lymphatic Drainage session appropriately.

Are you hiring?

We're always looking for licensed therapists to join our team. Email your resume to our business manager, Latosha Moss, at Latosha@massagewayhendersonville.com.
